# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

General public — locals and visitors discovering the coffee shop, browsing on mobile or desktop. Context: casual intent, often in-the-moment (nearby, looking for somewhere to go). Primary job: understand what this place is about and decide whether to visit.

## Product Purpose

Landing site for an urban coffee shop. Goal: convert curiosity into a visit. Success means the visitor leaves with a clear sense of the shop's identity and a reason to show up.

## Brand Personality

Bold, energetic, urban. Confident without being aggressive. The shop knows what it is and doesn't need to explain itself. Every element earns its presence.

## Anti-references

- Dark neon / crypto aesthetic — no glowing borders, no dark-mode spectacle for its own sake
- Generic café warmth — stock-photo latte art, earth-tone softness, Instagram-filter nostalgia
- Startup template look — SaaS-cream, gradient blobs, section-per-section scrollytelling with icons

## Design Principles

1. **Confident stillness** — bold energy expressed through restraint, not noise. Urgency through scale and weight, not animation or color overload.
2. **Clarity as respect** — visitors are busy. Information lands fast. No intros that repeat the headline.
3. **Urban texture** — the city is part of the brand. Design should feel like it belongs on a real street, not a mood board.
4. **Typography does the heavy lifting** — hierarchy through scale and weight contrast. Color plays a supporting role.
5. **Show the place, not the vibe** — concrete details (hours, location, what's on offer) matter more than atmospheric copy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Ensure sufficient contrast at all text sizes. Reduced motion support via `prefers-reduced-motion` for any animations.
