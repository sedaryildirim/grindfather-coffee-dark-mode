WE STARTED
WITH ONE
DRUM.

We started with a single drum roaster, a notebook of cupping scores, and a stubborn belief that great coffee should taste like the place it came from, not like itself. A decade on, we still roast every batch by ear.

Pulled on a 1969 Faema E61 we restored over two winters. Sourced from farmers we know by name and visit each year. Every bag ships within 72 hours of leaving the drum.
